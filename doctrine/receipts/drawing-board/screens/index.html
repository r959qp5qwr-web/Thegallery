The Gallery, as an app

Sixteen screens on a phone, in The Gallery's original look: spaced capitals, wall labels, the italic curator line and amber. App controls are added: a bottom bar, image grids, a heart, a fixed Message button and short sign-up steps. The photographs are invented objects, rendered for these drawings. The number beside each screen counts its words.
