# 01 · UX/UI audit lens (ux-ui-audit skill)

Method note: the skill's DOM probes (probe-core, probe-heuristics, probe-perception, probe-focus, probe-routes) were **not run**, because the brief forbids opening the live site in a browser. Everything below comes from the skill's screenshot pass and reading pass over the 29 captured pages at 390 / 820 / 1440, plus reading the source (`public/gallery/*.css`, `src/components/Chrome.tsx`, `src/app/(entrance)/page.tsx`) to name the cause where I could. Contrast figures come from the foundation's own token measurements, not from screenshots. Full-page captures show the fixed bottom bar painted mid-page. That is a capture artefact, not a defect, and no finding below relies on it.

## Verdict

The governor's "novice" reaction is correct, and it has three causes. First, **the live site is a chain of empty rooms that point at each other.** Every public empty state sends the visitor to another empty state, and each one uses the same beige box with a ledge in it. At 1440 that box is a 1104 × 168 px slab that reads like a broken image. Second, **the product repeats itself on every screen.** On the For Makers page at 390, "For Makers" appears four times in one viewport (header title, eyebrow, active tab, footer). The eyebrow "ONE GALLERY OF YOUR OWN" sits directly above body copy that opens "One gallery of your own." The same "no cart / the maker owns the transaction" sentence appears on About, For Makers, the maker gallery and the contact sheet. On the live entrance the CTA, the tab and the footer all say "For Makers", and two hairlines stack with nothing between them. Third, **the craft breaks in the details a design-literate eye catches first.** Examples: the header title sits 40 px off-centre; a typewriter wordmark is the same face as the status labels; native "Choose File / No file chosen" controls appear in the Studio; buttons truncate to "Remove 1…"; an email address breaks as "examp / le"; desktop grids centre their orphan tiles, so the hang looks unfinished. The token system is sound: the ivory, ink and amber palette and the line-not-block action are good foundations. But the pages built on it read as a careful wireframe, not an exhibition. On a phone, the one thing the product exists for, contacting the maker, is below the first screen of the work page.

## Moves (whole-product lifts)

1. **Never show a dead room.** Write one rule into page code: no link, rail tab or CTA may lead to a surface with zero works. On the live site the entrance should be a designed opening (a curatorial paragraph, the five materials as text and fragment, About), not a beige placeholder that points to five more placeholders. (U01, U02, U06)
2. **One statement, one place.** Name each screen once and state each idea once. Cut repeated header titles, eyebrows and footer links, and give each disclaimer one home. This alone removes most of the "duplication" the governor saw. (U04, U05, U18, U21)
3. **Give the brand its own voice.** The wordmark should be the only element in the header that is not a label (a serif or lettered mark). Hold any one line of metadata to a single register. **SYSTEM CHANGE** for the wordmark token. (U09, U10)
4. **Make contact reachable on the first screen of every work on a phone.** Shorten the compact image cap or add a sticky contact strip, and correct the 60 vh figure in INTERFACE_FOUNDATION §10. (U03)
5. **Design the expanded width rather than stretching the phone.** Use a left-aligned three-column hang with dense packing, nav sized for desktop, a two-column editorial layout for prose pages, and empty-state plinths sized to the column. (U07, U14, U02)
