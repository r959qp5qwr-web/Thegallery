# 05 — Words and the first-time journey

Reviewer lens: UX writing, UX copy and journey mapping (skills `design-skills:ux-writing`, `design:ux-copy`, `design-skills:journey-mapping`). Evidence: captures under `scratchpad/captures/` plus page code in `/workspace/Thegallery/src/app/**/page.tsx` and `src/components/Chrome.tsx`. Nothing was edited, built or opened live.

## Verdict

The words are why the site reads as "novice". They are mostly careful and honest one line at a time, but together they sound like a policy document and a system, not a gallery. Take a visitor arriving from a WhatsApp link today. The first words they read, "On view now", are contradicted by the next line, "No work is on view yet". The only button on the page is addressed to someone else ("For Makers"). Every route away from the page leads to another empty room: clay sends you to textile, textile to clay, Saved to "See all work" (which shows no work), and search to "Browse by material" (empty). Nothing says what The Gallery is, and nothing gives the visitor a reason to come back. The phone shows "For Makers" three times on one screen: as the page button, in the bottom bar and in the footer. The maker's journey is shorter but leans on internal words a potter has never been taught: "Studio", "transaction", "materially produced", "full international form", "lifecycle"-style "confirmed 5 days ago", and a price mode "On enquiry" that sits beside a status called "By enquiry". The same ideas are said three or four times across pages, often in the same words: "hosts the encounter / owns the transaction" appears on three surfaces, "Who may hold a gallery" is copied word for word between two pages, and "Nothing is public until you publish" appears twice on one form. Meanwhile the moments that should be said once and said well (what this place is, why a visitor should trust it, what happens after you sign up) are missing. The foundation itself is partly to blame. `INTERFACE_FOUNDATION.md` specifies the typography of the "curatorial voice" in detail but never defines the voice: no vocabulary, no banned words, no rule against repetition. The copy has drifted accordingly.

## Moves

1. **Write a one-page voice sheet and add it to the foundation.** It should include a glossary of permitted words (gallery, room, work/piece, maker, Studio, hang, open) and a list of banned words (transaction, materially, confirmed, process, admitted, lifecycle). Add one rule: *say each fact once, in the place it matters.* `INTERFACE_FOUNDATION.md` defines the typography of the "curatorial voice" but not the voice itself; that gap is how this copy drifted.
2. **Design the empty site as a launch moment, not a state.** While nothing is published, every visitor route (Entrance, Browse, Saved, Search, Workshops) should share one honest message: "The first works are being hung." Give it one action for visitors (what this is) and one quiet line for makers. No route should loop between empty rooms (W-01 to W-04, W-21).
3. **Separate the two audiences in the words.** Visitor surfaces never address makers except in one quiet line. Maker surfaces (For Makers, sign-up, Studio) speak plain benefits: free, no commission, WhatsApp, about 15 minutes. Introduce "Studio" before using it (W-02, W-06, W-07, W-17).
4. **Say each policy once.** Who may join and what The Gallery does not do belong on About and Participation, each said once. Everywhere else, link to them, or keep a single warm sentence at the contact moment (W-12 to W-15).
5. **One name per idea across Studio and public pages.** "On enquiry" and "By enquiry", "confirmed" and "updated", "label" (wall) and "label" (button text) need one name each. Route the price-versus-status overlap to the Governor as an open product decision (W-08, W-09, W-10, W-11).
